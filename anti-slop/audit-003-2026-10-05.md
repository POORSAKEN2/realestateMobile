# Profile navigation and foreground audit

Mode: after (session override). Scope: the requested Profile categories and text colors on Profile badges and the property Manage button. The user's request authorizes these fixes.

## Findings and resolutions

1. HIGH, R-25/R-34: primary purple badge text lacks sufficient contrast on pale backgrounds. Resolved with theme-specific `primaryContent`; filled brand buttons use `onPrimary` for both text and icons. Shared tokens update with the active theme.
2. MEDIUM, R-31: the combined Account & Organization heading obscures the requested separation. Renamed it Organization, added Account directly beneath it, and reused ProfileMenuSection for the Settings row. Support follows Account.
3. HIGH, R-03: adding another section to a fixed-height screen risks clipping content on shorter displays. Replaced the container with a ScrollView and retained clearance above bottom navigation.

## Design read and reasons

Reading this as Terrane's existing mobile account menu, preserving its Raleway typography, purple brand palette, rounded cards, and identity-card focal point. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Color: stronger purple on tinted badges and dark foreground on filled purple keep brand identity while meeting normal-text contrast.
- Layout: Organization, Account, and Support group the existing destinations and requested Settings shortcut by purpose.
- Typography and cards: reuse the shared menu component to preserve the existing hierarchy and touch targets.
- Spacing: scrolling and bottom padding keep every destination reachable above the tab bar.
- Icons: reuse the existing settings icon and navigation chevrons; no new assets or decoration.
